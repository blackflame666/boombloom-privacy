Privacy Policy

BoomBloom · Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how BoomBloom ("we", "us", "the game") handles your information. BoomBloom is developed by Randel Ramirez ("Delram Games") and is available on Google Play.

By installing and playing BoomBloom, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect directly

BoomBloom does not collect any personally identifiable information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. We do not require you to create an account.

Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked themes, and current theme) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your phone, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Information collected by third parties

BoomBloom uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded advertisements. When you choose to watch an ad (for example, to continue after a game over, double your coins, or reveal a hint), Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable, anonymous identifier assigned to your device
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address (typically city-level)
  • Device information — model, operating system version, language, and similar data
  • Ad interaction data — whether an ad was displayed, clicked, or dismissed

This data is used to serve relevant ads and to measure ad performance. Google may combine it with data collected from other apps and services to personalize ads.

We do not receive, store, or have access to any of the data AdMob collects on our behalf. All ad data handling is governed by Google's own privacy practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob and how it uses data: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • How to opt out of personalized ads: adssettings.google.com

3. How to opt out of personalized ads

You can control ad personalization through your Android device settings:

  • Open Settings → Google → Ads
  • Tap Delete advertising ID to reset it, or
  • Toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization on

You will still see ads in BoomBloom, but they will not be personalized based on your advertising ID.

4. Children's privacy

BoomBloom is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and up). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through our game, please contact us and we will delete it.

Parents: if you allow a child to play BoomBloom, we recommend disabling ads or using device-level parental controls to restrict in-app ad interactions.

5. Data retention and deletion

Since we do not collect any personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. All game progress is stored locally on your device and is fully removed when you uninstall BoomBloom.

To delete the advertising ID associated with your device, follow the instructions in section 3 above.

6. Security

We do not transmit any data from your device ourselves. AdMob encrypts all communication with Google's servers using industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Continued use of BoomBloom after any update means you accept the new policy.
